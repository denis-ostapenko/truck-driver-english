# Cycle 3 Final Remediation

## Verdict and scope

This document describes the current local Truck Driver English release candidate. It supersedes the Cycle 1 and Cycle 2 remediation notes as a runtime description. It does not certify a driver, predict a roadside inspection outcome, establish professional competence, or assign CEFR or ACTFL level.

The application remains fully local. Cycle 3 did not publish, deploy or commit the project, did not use a paid API, and did not use a real microphone. Browser verification used a separate isolated origin and profile. Origin 8002 was not read, changed or cleared.

## Honest limitations

- This is a local release candidate, not external professional certification or regulatory approval.
- Diagnostic forms follow the same explicit blueprint but have not undergone an external reliability, validity or equivalence study.
- Typed recall, local listening checks, document reading and simulated interaction do not prove oral fluency, field performance or job readiness.
- Automated DSP, hashes and metadata can verify audio production integrity, but final human perceptual listening remains an external QA task.
- Regulatory and policy content is current to the source review date in this cycle and must be checked again before a later release.
