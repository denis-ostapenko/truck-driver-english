# Терминология Truck Driver English

Ниже находится стартовый P1 inventory: 120 терминов и 30 речевых формул. Это не весь Truck Driver Track 400. Остальные units добавляются после проверки дублей с General Core 700 и распределения по 32 ситуациям.

## 30 обязательных речевых формул

### Official contact, 10

1. `Yes, officer. I understand.`
2. `Where would you like me to stop?`
3. `The parking brake is set.`
4. `The engine is off.`
5. `May I exit the vehicle now?`
6. `May I reach for the document folder?`
7. `I will remain in the cab.`
8. `I will not move the vehicle.`
9. `Am I clear to proceed?`
10. `What is the next required step?`

### Documents and trip facts, 10

1. `Here is my commercial driver's license.`
2. `Here is the tractor registration.`
3. `The trailer registration is in this folder.`
4. `Here is the proof of insurance.`
5. `The periodic inspection documentation is on the vehicle.`
6. `Here is the bill of lading for this load.`
7. `I picked up in [city, state].`
8. `I am delivering in [city, state].`
9. `I am hauling [commodity].`
10. `My current duty status is [status].`

### Clarification and repair, 10

1. `Could you repeat that more slowly?`
2. `Could you rephrase the question?`
3. `I understood the first part, but not the second part.`
4. `Are you asking for the tractor or trailer registration?`
5. `Do you want me to show the screen or transfer the records?`
6. `Which transfer method would you like?`
7. `May I check the document before I answer?`
8. `I do not know that information.`
9. `I do not want to guess. The document shows [fact].`
10. `May I repeat the instruction to confirm it?`

## Правила переноса в приложение

- Каждый abbreviation unit хранит полную форму, spoken form и значение.
- Safety-critical term имеет один канонический перевод и контекст.
- Синонимы добавляются как recognition variants, если отдельное активное изучение не нужно.
- Equipment term связывается с точным hotspot на схеме.
- Official phrase связывается с ролью, inspection level и success condition.
- Перед включением выполняется duplicate check против General Core 700.
