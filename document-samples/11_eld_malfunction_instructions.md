## ELD Malfunction Instruction Sheet

Training sequence:

1. Note the malfunction code and time.
2. Notify the motor carrier in writing within 24 hours after discovering the malfunction.
3. Reconstruct the current 24-hour period and previous seven consecutive days when required and not otherwise retrievable.
4. Continue to prepare manual paper RODS until the ELD is serviced and brought back into compliance.
5. Keep the reconstructed records available for a roadside inspection.
6. The motor carrier normally must repair, replace or service the ELD within eight days after discovering the condition or receiving the driver's notice, whichever occurs first, unless FMCSA grants an extension under 49 CFR 395.34.
